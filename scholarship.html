Undergraduate Scholarship & Mentorship

Scholarship Support. Christian Mentorship. A Stronger Journey to Graduation.

The De_Light Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship & Mentorship Programme supports Nigerian undergraduates who need financial assistance to continue their education. Alongside scholarship support, selected students will have access to mentorship and guidance as they navigate university life and prepare for the future.

Apply Now Support a Student
How the Programme Works

What we provide

1

Scholarship Support

Financial assistance towards school fees for eligible undergraduate students. Awards are subject to available funding and the Foundation's selection process.

2

Mentorship Structure

Each selected scholar will be assigned to a mentor, with approximately two to three scholars per mentor. Mentorship will involve periodic virtual meetings and ongoing communication.

3

Continued Guidance

Mentorship discussions will focus on spiritual growth, academic progress, personal development, challenges and living out one's faith on campus.

4

Preparation for Graduation

We hope to organise an annual post-graduation seminar to help final-year students and recent graduates prepare for employment and life beyond university.

Who We Serve

Christian undergraduates, with room to serve beyond

Our primary focus is on Christian undergraduate students in Nigerian tertiary institutions. Christian applicants will receive priority, as the programme seeks to strengthen the Christian community on Nigerian campuses. However, we recognise that the need for educational support extends beyond religious boundaries — a limited number of non-Christian students may also be considered. All applicants must demonstrate genuine financial need, commitment to their education and a willingness to participate responsibly in the programme.

Eligibility

Application requirements

  • Be currently enrolled as an undergraduate student in a recognised Nigerian tertiary institution.
  • Demonstrate genuine financial need.
  • Submit a motivation letter explaining your background, educational goals, financial circumstances and reasons for applying.
  • Provide an academic transcript, where applicable. Applicants in 200 level and above should provide their transcripts; those yet to receive one should indicate this in their application.
  • Provide two recommendation letters — one from an academic referee, and one from a church/religious leader or appropriate community leader.
How to Apply

Application form

Applications are collected through our online form. Please have your motivation letter, transcript (where applicable) and recommendation letters ready to attach before you begin.
