Our Programs

Supporting Young People in Faith, Education and Personal Development

De_Light Foundation is committed to strengthening the younger generation through faith, education, mentorship and practical opportunities. Our current focus is the Undergraduate Scholarship and Mentorship Programme, while we continue working towards making practical technology education accessible to more young Nigerians.

Program 01 · Current Focus

Undergraduate Scholarship & Mentorship Programme

Scholarship support, Christian mentorship, and guidance all the way to graduation — for Nigerian undergraduates who need it most.

Program 02 · Planned Initiative

Technology Skills Acquisition Programme

Equipping young people for a changing digital world. We believe young people should have access to practical technology skills that prepare them for opportunities in employment, entrepreneurship and the digital economy. Building on De_Light Foundation's original focus on technology education, we envision a training programme covering five key areas:

1

AI Tools & Applications

Introducing practical applications of artificial intelligence for learning, creativity and productivity.

2

Video Creation & Editing

Developing skills in creating, editing and communicating ideas through video.

3

Creative Thinking & Content Writing

Encouraging creativity, clear thinking and effective communication.

4

Data Analysis

Introducing practical approaches to understanding data and using it to inform decisions.

5

Graphics Design

Building foundational visual design skills and exploring tools for bringing creative ideas to life.

Our Plans

Subject to sufficient funding and available resources, we hope to organise an intensive training programme supported by access to learning equipment and structured mentorship. This initiative is part of our long-term vision and will commence when the necessary resources are available. We welcome partnerships and support from individuals and organisations who share this vision.
